# SkinClinic Product Catalogue — C-Aesthetics Phase 1

## 1. Exoboost

**Positioning:** Exosome and PDRN activating serum  
**Format:** 30 ml serum  
**Use:** Advanced home care / professionally guided routine

### Shopify description

Exoboost is the activating first step in SkinClinic's regenerative routine. The concentrated serum combines exosome technology and PDRN with hydrating and barrier-supportive ingredients to support skin communication, recovery and a more revitalised appearance. It is designed to be followed by Exoday Cream in the morning or Exonight Cream in the evening.

### Why choose it

- Supports the skin's natural renewal and recovery processes.
- Prepares the routine for the complementary Exoday or Exonight moisturiser.
- Combines advanced regenerative positioning with hydration and barrier care.

### How to use

Apply a small amount to clean, dry skin in the morning and/or evening. Distribute evenly until absorbed, then follow with Exoday Cream or Exonight Cream according to the time of day. For use around aesthetic procedures, follow the treating professional's instructions.

**Ingredient highlights:** Exocell-Longevity®, squalane, Cutiguard CLR™, Epitensive™, Shonai Bijin™, hyaluronic acid.

## 5. Melanyc

**Positioning:** Intensive night care for uneven-looking pigmentation  
**Format:** 50 ml cream  
**Use:** Home care; consider professional guidance for reactive skin

### Shopify description

Melanyc is an intensive evening cream developed for skin affected by visible dark spots and uneven tone. Its multi-acid and brightening complex combines kojic, phytic and glycolic acids with arbutin, niacinamide, vitamin C and botanical extracts. Used as part of a consistent night routine, it helps refine the look of discolouration and promote a more even, luminous complexion.

### Why choose it

- Targets the appearance of stubborn, localised discolouration.
- Combines exfoliating, antioxidant and tone-supporting ingredients.
- Designed as a focused night step rather than a general moisturiser.

### How to use

Apply a small amount in the evening to clean, dry skin, following the approved label directions. Introduce gradually if you are new to exfoliating acids. Avoid the eye area and compromised skin. Use broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning. Do not layer with other strong exfoliants or retinoids unless advised by a qualified professional.

**Ingredient highlights:** Kojic acid, phytic acid, vitamin C, glycolic acid, arbutin, niacinamide, liquorice extract, Chromabright®.

## 12. Retiplus

**Positioning:** Retinal night cream for visible texture, lines and loss of firmness  
**Format:** 50 ml cream  
**Use:** Advanced home care

### Shopify description

Retiplus is an intensive night cream combining encapsulated retinal with retinyl palmitate, ceramides, ectoin, stable vitamin C and supportive peptides. It is designed to improve the look of texture, fine lines and firmness while pairing vitamin A activity with barrier-supportive ingredients. This is an advanced formula and may not suit sensitive skin.

### Why choose it

- Targets multiple visible signs of skin ageing.
- Encapsulated retinal provides a focused vitamin A night step.
- Ceramides and ectoin complement the active formula with barrier support.

### How to use

Introduce slowly at night according to the approved label, beginning with limited weekly use. Apply to dry skin and avoid the eye area, corners of the nose and lips. **Ingredient highlights:** Retinyl palmitate, encapsulated retinal, ceramides, ectoin, stable vitamin C, MPC™ milk peptide complex, Gatuline®, avocado oil, moringa oil.
