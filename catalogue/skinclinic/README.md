# SkinClinic Shopify Catalogue — Phase 1

Prepared for C-Aesthetics by Curative Pte. Ltd.

This package contains a launch-ready first catalogue of fourteen SkinClinic products selected across exosome-led regeneration, cleansing, pigmentation, sensitive-skin, antioxidant, hydration, age-support and blemish-care needs.

## Files

- `skinclinic-shopify-draft-import.csv` — Shopify-compatible draft product import.
- `skinclinic-product-copy.md` — editable product-page copy, usage directions, ingredient highlights and source references.
- `source-manifest.csv` — source pages, original EUR reference prices and image provenance.
- `images/` — fourteen manufacturer product images sourced from Curative's shared Google Drive and the live SkinClinic catalogue.

## Import notes

1. Import the CSV into Shopify Products.
2. Keep every item in **Draft** status until Singapore pricing, inventory, regulatory copy and availability are approved.
3. The image URLs point to Curative's shared Drive assets. If Shopify cannot retrieve a Drive image, upload the matching JPEG from `images/` to that product.
4. Confirm complete INCI lists from current packaging or approved distributor documents before publication. The catalogue lists only highlighted ingredients, not full INCI declarations.
5. Review directions, contraindications and claims against the exact Singapore-market formulation and HSA/advertising requirements.

## Editorial approach

The descriptions are newly written for C-Aesthetics. They summarise the manufacturer's product positioning without reproducing the SkinClinic website verbatim. Cosmetic language such as “helps”, “supports” and “appearance of” is used deliberately; no product is presented as diagnosing, treating or curing disease.
